MOFChat · Team 09 · 4

A question goes through five stages. The planner turns it into a structured JSON plan, never code. The validator checks the plan and sends errors back to the LLM for up to two corrections, then falls back to the rule parser. The executor runs one of four tools. The LLM then writes the answer from the verified rows only, and the answer validator rejects any MOF ID, number or comparison claim the results do not support. The app always shows the verified table next to the text.
