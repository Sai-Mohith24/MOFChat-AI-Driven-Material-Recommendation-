The FT-Transformer follows Gorishniy et al. 2021: each descriptor becomes a token, metal and open-metal-site are categorical tokens, and a [CLS] token summarises the MOF. Raw k-NN optimises exactly the descriptor-gap metric, so it is an upper bound there; the embedding is close to it while matching the metal more often, because the metal is an input token. For knowledge questions, curated notes with references cover dataset definitions, kinetic diameters, application heuristics, MOF basics and our own methods. The right source is first for 75 percent of 12 test questions and in the top three for 83 percent; all five off-topic questions, such as a chocolate cake recipe, are declined.
