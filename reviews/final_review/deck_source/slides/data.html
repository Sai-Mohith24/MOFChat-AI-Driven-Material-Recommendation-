setup_db.py loads the spreadsheet, renames columns to a clean schema and asserts each of these facts, so they are tested, not just observed. The zero-surface-area MOFs are real, not missing data: the descriptors were computed with a 3.31 angstrom nitrogen probe, and a pore limiting diameter below that means no accessible surface. The result is a SQLite database with a materials table, a normalised metal table and column documentation.
