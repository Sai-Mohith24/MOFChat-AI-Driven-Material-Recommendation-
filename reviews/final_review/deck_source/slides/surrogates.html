Features: density, LCD, PLD, LFPD, void fraction and pore volume; split 70/10/10/10 for training, early stopping, conformal calibration and test. XGBoost and the FT-Transformer are statistically tied, which matches Grinsztajn et al.: trees remain very strong on medium-sized tabular data. XGBoost trains about 400 times faster and gives exact TreeSHAP explanations, so it is the production model and the FT-Transformer is a second opinion. SHAP ranks pore volume and void fraction first, which is physically sensible. Void fraction is also predicted, with R squared 0.828.
