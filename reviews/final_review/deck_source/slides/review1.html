Say this openly: we tested our own review-1 system and it failed. A two-tower model needs contrastive training on question and MOF pairs; equal dimensions do not make two vector spaces comparable. Numeric constraints are facts, so we now evaluate them exactly in SQL and let the language model do what it is good at, understanding the question. The FT-Transformer stayed where it is valid: MOF-to-MOF similarity and property prediction. The benchmark that shows the failure is part of the project.
