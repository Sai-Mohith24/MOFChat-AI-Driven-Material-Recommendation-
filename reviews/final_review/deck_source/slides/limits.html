Limitations and future work

What MOFChat cannot do yet

No adsorption data

Application answers are geometric heuristics. Next: attach GCMC uptakes for hMOF/CoRE and train the same surrogates on them.

Recommendation only

No structure generation, unlike ChatMOF's genetic algorithm.

Unusual phrasings

Some held-out questions are still mis-planned; LoRA fine-tuning on plan examples would help.

Free-text reasoning

The validator checks IDs, numbers and comparisons, not a wrong formula in a sentence.

Be direct about these. The dataset has geometry only, so we never claim adsorption performance; the hMOF folder has structures and energy grids but no uptake labels. Generation is out of scope.
