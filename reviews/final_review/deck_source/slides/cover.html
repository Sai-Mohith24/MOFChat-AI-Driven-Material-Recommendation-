MOFChat

Explainable, data-grounded recommendation of metal-organic frameworks with validated LLM planning, multi-objective optimisation and surrogate models

Team 09

Amrutha T (24255) · Charan Tej Reddy Y (24259) · Poojitha D (24263) · Enku Sai Mohith (24316) · Ramu Jahna Bindu (24249)

MOFChat answers plain-English questions about metal-organic frameworks over the 12,020-MOF CoRE-MOF 2019 table, the dataset behind the ChatMOF paper. The language model never invents data: every MOF and every number comes from the database, and every answer is checked before it is shown.
